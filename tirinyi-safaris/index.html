    Before you travel

    When is the best time to visit?

    Uganda is a year-round destination. The drier months, December to February and June to August, are the easiest for trekking and game drives.

    Do I need a visa?

    Most visitors do, and you apply online through the official Uganda e-Visa portal. Some regional visitors are exempt, so check before you travel.

    How do gorilla permits work?

    Permits are issued by the Uganda Wildlife Authority and sell out in busy months. We book yours as soon as you confirm, so enquire early.

    What should I pack for trekking?

    Waterproof hiking boots, long sleeves and trousers, a light rain jacket, garden gloves for grabbing vines, and a small daypack.

    How do I pay?

    A deposit confirms your trip and permits. The balance is due before you travel, by bank transfer, card or mobile money.
